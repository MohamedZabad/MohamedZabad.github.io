About

I'm a university student who learns by wiring things up and breaking them on purpose. Most of my work sits at the point where software meets something physical or something else on the network — a sensor on a breadboard, a socket between two machines, a model doing the boring part of a job.

I like projects with a real feedback loop: flash the firmware, watch the serial monitor, fix the wiring, try again. Below is a working log of what that's produced so far.
